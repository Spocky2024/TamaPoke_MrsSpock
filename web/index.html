🖖 TamaPoke 5.92 Mrs. Spock - Version - Germany

A 386-Pokémon tamagotchi for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75. Install it from your browser — no Arduino, no drivers.

⚠️ Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Flash the firmware

Plug the board in via USB and press install. Pick the port and wait.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

🔄 Updating to a new version? Install without erasing to keep your Pokémon — the save lives in flash and the sprites on the microSD, so a normal install keeps both. Only a full Erase wipes your saved pet (the SD sprites survive even that). First time on a fresh board? You can erase, no problem.

2Load the sprites onto the microSD

Put a microSD into the board (it formats itself). Press Connect board, then load the three sprite packs one after another — about 136 MB in total, roughly 20 minutes. Don't remove the card.

Close the step-1 install tab first (only one program can use the port at a time).

⏱️ The board switches itself off after 5 minutes without a touch, even on USB. Tap its screen every few minutes while loading. If a pack gets interrupted anyway (or the board switched off), switch it on again with PWR, press Connect board and press the button of that pack only: it continues where it stopped, and the other packs don't have to be repeated.
